Thesis (branch claude-research)

Owner direction, 2026-10-30. This branch follows the owner's federation model. It does not follow the "Path A, YC is not the scoreboard" text in principles.md on main; main is untouched.

Model

A company made of vertical companies. Each vertical is run by one founder with agents as staff, owns one channel or craft, and has already won on its own. Two to four such founders found one company together. Example: A = social media, B = paid ads, C = SEO, D = email. Joined, they are a full-service agency that can face the big holding companies, with a fraction of the headcount.

Why it can fit YC: the unit is small and proven (a vertical with revenue), the combined company has a new cost structure (agents instead of staff), and a clear claim ("the agency that is 3 people and does the work of 300").

What this branch has to answer

  1. Which verticals exist in a full-service agency, and for each one: who already wins with an agent-native or software-first model, at what revenue, at what price, where is the gap.
  2. Which vertical the owner should lead. Test from principles.md (Filter): the owner judges whether the output is peak, the product scores/selects/directs and does not make the creative by hand, demand is visible, a paid micro-offer can be sold this month.
  3. What the other verticals need to look like, so the owner knows which co-founders to look for. Not a recruiting task for the research.
  4. What the combined company claims, and the first proof of it.

Scope decisions (from the owner)

  • Market: global winners first, local (Vietnam / Southeast Asia) after.
  • Buyers: brand, celebrity, agency.
  • Sources for winners: TrustMRR (verified MRR), YC, a16z Speedrun; Product Hunt and Hacker News ranked below and marked unverified (master-winning-product).

Scope correction (owner, 2026-10-30)

Social media, paid ads, SEO and email were examples of what a vertical looks like, taken from the verticals the owner has worked in. They are not the search space. The search is for a NEW winning product in any industry, digital or not. research/social-media.md, paid-ads.md, seo.md, email.md stay as reference data from a first, too narrow pass.

How the filter changes: "the owner can judge whether the output is peak" is read as "a domain where the owner's judgment, deep research, strategy and AI-native workflow give an edge", not "marketing only". Demand is proven by what already earns (verified MRR) or is funded (YC, Speedrun).

Sweep method: by source across all categories, not by channel.

  1. TrustMRR: verified-MRR winners with tiny teams or one founder, any category, 2025-2026.
  2. YC: recent batches, AI-agent and vertical-agent companies, with what they sell and to whom.
  3. a16z Speedrun and others: same, plus indie sales markets (Microns, Acquire, Flippa) as evidence of what small products sell for. Then cluster the rows into themes and score each theme against the filter.

Rules for the research

  • Numbers come with a source link and a date. Not readable means blank, never a guess.
  • Unverified rows never rank above verified ones.
  • Each finding carries a confidence label.
