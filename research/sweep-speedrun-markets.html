Market reading (confidence: medium, asking prices)

  • Small software with $1.4K to $50K monthly revenue lists at roughly 2.4x to 4.8x annual profit; median of the 20 SaaS rows with a stated multiple is about 3.2x. Outliers: 0.9x (consumer dating app), 5.1x to 5.8x (trading, Gmail tool).
  • Content assets (newsletters, YouTube, KDP) list at 1.3x to 3.6x; media is cheaper per dollar of profit than SaaS.
  • True micro sales (Microns) clear at $2K to about $2.5K on $90 to $230 revenue, so the floor for an unproven micro product is near-zero; value comes from proven profit.
  • Acquire.com secondary benchmark (beancount.io, July 2026, a single blog, low confidence): sub-$1M ARR sells at 2.5x to 4x revenue; AI-first is said to be 3 of 4 listings (podcast summary).
  • Winning small products here: AI tools for a defined profession (nursing students, teachers, local SEO agencies, captioning, voice agents), profit margins 60 to 90 percent, owner time near zero.

Cross-sweep take (judgment, not data)

Both sources point at (a) marketing/media/creator tooling and content businesses, where the owner's skills apply directly and small proven products sell at about 3x profit, and (b) vertical AI services for a profession where deep research of one workflow is the moat. Speedrun shows funded demand but no verified revenue; the markets show price but only asking. Neither is a verified-MRR source: pair with the TrustMRR sweep before ranking.
