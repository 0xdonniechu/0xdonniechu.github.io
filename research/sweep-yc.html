Sweep: Y Combinator, 2026 batches vs 2025 (branch claude-research)

Date pulled: 2026-10-30. Confidence label on every finding below: HIGH = counted directly from the data, MEDIUM = keyword proxy, LOW = my reading.

Method and limits

  • Batches the YC directory shows as of today: Fall 2026, Summer 2026, Spring 2026, Winter 2026, then Fall/Summer/Spring/Winter 2025. There is no "X26" or "P26" batch (a lookup for it returned 404).
  • Browser tool: none available. The directory page loads by JavaScript and returns no listing to a plain fetch. I used the public JSON mirror of the YC directory (yc-oss.github.io/api/batches/<batch>.json), which carries name, one-liner, long description, team size, industry, sub-industry, website, status. Then I fetched each chosen company's own page on ycombinator.com for the founder list. Mirror snapshot date is unknown; batch sizes (W26 197, S26 193, Su26 229, F26 192, F25 145, Su25 166, Sp25 144, W25 165) may be partial, so counts are indicative.
  • Sample: 57 rows hand-picked from about 1,430 companies across 8 batches: AI-agent / vertical-agent / AI-native-firm companies, team size 5 or fewer except Contrario (20) and Risely AI (8), status Active, skipping developer-infrastructure and agent-tooling companies (they sell to other builders, not to a vertical buyer). The pick is mine, so theme counts describe the sample, not the market.
  • Funding: blank on every row. YC profile text does not state amounts. Not guessed.
  • Revenue: only what a company wrote on its own YC profile, quoted, unverified. 13 of the 57 rows have such a line (a 14th, TrueMile, quotes the founder's earlier trucking business, not the company). Nothing here is TrustMRR-grade; per the method, unverified never ranks above verified.
  • Row order is by batch (newest first), not by rank, because there is no verified revenue to rank by.

What shifted, in order of how sure I am:

  1. Teams got smaller. Half of the 2026 companies list 1-2 people, up from about a third. (HIGH)
  2. Dev-tool share fell by about half (15% to 8%) while Industrials rose (10% to 19%), led by manufacturing/robotics, defense, energy: the batch moved from software for engineers toward physical industries. Summer 2026 is the extreme: 25% Industrials. (HIGH)
  3. "Agent" as a word did not grow (40% both years), so agents are the baseline, not the differentiator. What grew is the AI-native firm that sells the service itself (law firm, accounting firm, brokerage, ad agency, radiology practice, trucking company, engineering firm): 8 companies in 2025, 30 in 2026, 15 of them in Summer 2026 alone. (MEDIUM: regex on one-liners, undercounts firms that describe themselves otherwise)
  4. A new sub-layer of "infrastructure for agents" (VMs, wallets, identity, evals, sandboxes, MCP) is visible in 2026 lists; 2026 Infrastructure + Security rose 9% to 13%. I excluded these from the rows on purpose. (HIGH for the counts, LOW for the reading)
  5. Revenue lines on profiles did not rise as a share. Founders are not showing more revenue; they are showing fewer people. (MEDIUM)

For the thesis: the 2026 shift matches "a company that is 3 people doing the work of 300" and also means that the claim is no longer unusual inside YC. T1 below has 15 sample rows and several are already near the agency model.
