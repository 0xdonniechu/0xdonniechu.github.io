Reading the sweep (confidence: medium; one snapshot, profile text is founder-written)

  • Marked as edge themes: A, D, E (and B/C only for the distribution side). They are where the buyer pays for a better decision or better output that a researcher with media judgment can verify.
  • Largest verified small-team numbers: GojiberryAI ($424k, founded 2025-07, Stripe), Postiz ($329k, +47.6% in 30 days), PROSP ($128k, founded 2025-08, 80% margin).
  • Fastest 30-day growth among rows: Audn.AI +89.4% (small base), Kibu +52.8%, Postiz +47.6%, AEO Engine +39.5%, Boothie +34.5%.
  • Caveats: many rows are listed for sale (seller incentive); 5 rows hide their name; team size is mostly unstated, so 'tiny team' is inferred from the profile, not confirmed. Kibu, AEO Engine, RankAI were founded before 2025 and are in as large reference points.
  • Not covered: the remaining ~4,300 listings, the Advertising platforms and OpenClaw special categories, E-commerce category (page returned no list).
