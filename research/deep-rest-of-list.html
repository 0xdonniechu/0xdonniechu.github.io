Cross-party read for DF (inference, marked as such)

  1. Billing and cancellation trust is the largest complaint theme across the parties read: Zeely 6 of 12 negatives, Marblism 4 of 10, Plai 1 of 3 (plus earlier Blaze 4 of 7). Inference: a plain "one price, cancel in one click, refund rules in writing" line is a product feature here.
  2. Metering confusion (credits or hours) is the second theme: Sintra 6 of 10, Marblism 3 of 10 (plus earlier Blaze, Sintra). A fixed monthly post count avoids it.
  3. Support that cannot fix things: Plai 2 of 3, Zeely 2 of 12, Marblism 4 of 10, Sintra 2 of 10. The praise side mirrors it: Plai (5+ of 8) and Marblism (earlier file) are praised for reachable named humans. Keep that.
  4. Output quality and repetition ("same topics over and over", "slop") appears at Marblism, Zeely and Sintra. DF's fact-check and voice training answer it only if the output proves it.
  5. Approval before publish: only Mizo (strategy approval then auto-publish) and Marblism (vendor claim) state anything; none describes a phone one-tap. Confidence low, since most approval flows were not read.
  6. Price anchors buyers may quote against DF: Mizo EUR 99 for 200 static posts; Marblism $24-44 for all employees; Sintra $15.60-97; Plai Done For You from $3,000. DF's $299 / $599 sits between self-serve AI at under $100 and human ghostwriting at $499-$3,999 (earlier file).
