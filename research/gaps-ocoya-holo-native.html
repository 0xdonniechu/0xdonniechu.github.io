Gaps in Ocoya, Holo and Native (customer-voice research)

Date of research: 2026-10-31. Method: public pages fetched via WebFetch/WebSearch; no sign-ups, no payment. Confidence labels: [HIGH] = several independent first-hand reviews or the vendor page itself; [MED] = a few reviews or one platform; [LOW] = one review or aggregator text.

Limits of this research (read first)

  • Reddit is blocked for our fetch tool: zero Reddit threads read. X and Product Hunt: nothing usable found (Product Hunt "Native" is an unrelated SwiftUI template, 0 reviews). Native.no has only 13 Trustpilot reviews and no G2/Capterra page found. So Native evidence is thin.
  • G2 and Capterra pages returned 403; Ocoya data from them is a search-engine summary (secondary, [MED] at best). Their rating of 4.6 (G2 171 reviews, Capterra 367 reviews, per search summary, not date-stamped) is partly incentivised per reviewers; treat as biased upward.
  • Trustpilot samples are self-selected and skew negative (Ocoya, Holo page 1 1-star filter); Trustpilot says Ocoya and Native have not recently invited reviews.
  • Counts below are counts inside the samples I could read, not market-wide rates.
  • Prior report (BYFA-skills/research/native-competitive-2026-10-26.html) lists Native Pro $79, Max $249, Agency $499 list and could not confirm Holo pricing; this file fills Holo pricing.

4. Needs customers ask for that none of the three has (inferred from complaints; label: [INFERENCE] unless noted)

  1. Transparent cost per output and no charge for the tool's own errors (free regeneration/fix; show credits before running). Holo complaints (11 of 20) explicitly; Native's tokens are unexplained on its page; Ocoya has credits too. None offers an error-refund guarantee. [HIGH for demand, from Holo; INFERENCE for the gap]
  2. Honest, easy trial and cancellation with a try-before-pay path (Ocoya billing after cancel; Holo no trial and refund refused; Native review: refund requests). All three require a card or no free path per pages read. [MED]
  3. Posts that provably publish with visible per-post status, retry and alerting (Ocoya). None of the three advertises a delivery guarantee. [MED]
  4. Output tied to the creator's real voice and product, not generic (Native, Holo). Nobody shows a measurable "sounds like me" check. [MED]
  5. Reuse of the creator's own long-form content (repurposing video/podcast into clips). None of the three's pricing pages lists repurposing from a long source as a core feature. [INFERENCE, not verified; the Repurpose.io half of the owner's product]
  6. Keep your content on cancel / export (Holo review). [LOW]
  7. Earning layer for creators (sponsorship, product links, revenue tracking). None sells it; no customer voice asked for it in my sample. [INFERENCE, LOW, flagged as the owner's own thesis, not customer evidence]
  8. Mobile-capable use (Ocoya "not very mobile friendly", Holo desktop-only). [LOW-MED]

5. Table stakes (all three or two of three have; we must match)

Source: vendor pages fetched 2026-10-31 and prior report. [HIGH unless stated]

  • Brand profile learned from a URL or brand kit (Holo Brand DNA, Native brand learning, Ocoya brand/design studio).
  • AI caption/post generation plus calendar scheduling and multi-network publishing (Ocoya 28 integrations; Native 11 channels; Holo IG/Meta/LinkedIn/TikTok/Pinterest/Facebook).
  • Image creation/editing studio (Ocoya Design Studio, Holo Studio, Native images).
  • Approval/review step before posting, multi-brand workspaces, team seats or agency tier.
  • Credits or tokens metering of AI use (all three).
  • Autopilot or recurring automated planning (Native core; Holo on Scale; Ocoya Workflows).
  • Basic analytics (Ocoya lower tier basic; Holo Scale only).
  • Integrations surface for agents/APIs (Ocoya API and MCP).
  • Multi-language (Holo claims 99+; others not checked [LOW]).

6. Ranked opportunities (top 10)

Ranking: verified evidence strength first, then count, then fit with the owner's creator-income goal.

  1. Reliable publishing with delivery guarantees (per-post status, auto-retry, alerts, account-health checks). Evidence: Ocoya failures on Trustpilot (3 of 6 non-billing 1-star), AppSumo (3 of 5), G2/Capterra. Confidence HIGH. Table stakes done well beats feature lists.
  2. No-surprise billing: real free tier or trial without card, one-click cancel, prorated refunds, data export. Evidence: Ocoya billing wave (Trustpilot 1.3 on 228), Holo refund refusals (about 4 of 20), Native refund asks. HIGH.
  3. Predictable pricing: flat or per-post limits, price shown per action before running; never charge for fixing the tool's errors. Evidence: Holo credits 11 of 20 + 3 low-star; Native tokens undefined on page; Ocoya price jumps. HIGH for Holo, MED overall.
  4. Output that fits the creator's voice and offer (train on past posts, show side-by-side with the creator's best posts, edit feedback learned). Evidence: Native 4 of 13, Holo quality 14 of 20, "no better than ChatGPT". MED-HIGH.
  5. Real support (human, fast, status page). Evidence: support appears in all three: Ocoya 4+ citations, Holo 13 of 20, Native 2. HIGH, but cheap to promise and costly to keep.
  6. Repurposing built in: long video/podcast/blog into shorts, carousels, threads, then schedule. Evidence: absent from all three pricing pages; Ocoya reviewers call it "a planning tool". INFERENCE, needs customer interviews. This is where Repurpose.io-style value is untouched.
  7. Honest AI: show what is generated and what is a template; avoid "wrapper" feel; benchmark against raw ChatGPT/Claude. Evidence: Native ("Claude api wrapper"), Holo ("not better than ChatGPT"), Ocoya ("zero AI"). MED.
  8. Complete channel coverage that actually works (TikTok, IG video, Google Business) with a public status of each integration. Evidence: AppSumo/G2/Capterra, undated. MED.
  9. Mobile and fast web app (Ocoya mobile, Holo desktop-only). LOW-MED; cheap relative to value for creators who work on phones.
  10. Creator income layer: link-in-bio sales, sponsor/affiliate tracking, post-to-revenue attribution. Evidence: no customer voice found; it is the owner's differentiator and nobody in the three sells it (Ocoya has bio pages only). INFERENCE, LOW; validate by interviews before building.
