3. Cross-product read-through (my synthesis, medium confidence)

  • Postiz wins on breadth (34 platforms), openness, agent surface and automations; loses on connect reliability, polish and mobile, and support consistency. Its pain is heaviest where it asks users to own OAuth apps (self-host) or where token refresh fails (YouTube, IG, LinkedIn, X).
  • Post Bridge wins on simplicity and price clarity for solo creators; its main evidence base is curated praise, so its true weak spots are unmeasured. Gaps vs Postiz: fewer platforms, no automations (plugs, RSS, repeats), no clipping, no open source, base plan has no team seats.
  • Shared bet: both sell "agent-ready scheduling" (MCP, CLI, API); Postiz reviewers praise drafts-first control.

Confidence summary: revenue figures high; Postiz pricing high; Postiz complaints themes A-C high/medium, D-F medium/low; Postiz praise medium; Post Bridge pricing/features high (vendor), Post Bridge complaints low (absence of evidence), praise low-medium (curated).
