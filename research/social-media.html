3. Answers

(1) Where is the gap. Medium confidence.

  • Money is verified in scheduling and posting (Postiz $333k MRR on $29 plans, Podawaa $63k). Scheduling is crowded, open source and cheap. Not a place to lead.
  • Money is verified in creator marketplaces (Stack Influence, $25k MRR, $29M all-time), which sell the workflow of buying creator posts.
  • The decision before the money goes out (which creator, which cut, which post to boost) is where funding is going (Clouted, Gudea, Kuli) but verified paying customers are not visible. The one cheap consumer scorer on TrustMRR has $2 of revenue. So demand for "score before you post" is claimed by investors and not yet proven by small buyers.
  • Brands, agencies and celebrity teams do not have an independent judge of "is this peak" they can buy by the piece. Scheduling tools answer when; they do not answer what.

(2) Gap for someone who judges virality and does not make creative. Medium confidence. Sell the judgment as a ranked pick, not a tool: a human-reviewed score across a batch of candidate posts/clips/creators, with a pick and the reason. Buyers: celebrity and brand teams with many drafts and one slot. This avoids the generation race (Clouted, Adobe) and the scheduler race (Postiz). Risk: Viral Validator shows a self-serve $9 scorer does not sell; the buyer who pays has spend or reputation at stake (brand, agency, celebrity), so sell to them at a higher price, with the owner's name on the call. Also CreatorML is inactive, so model-only prediction has failed once.

(3) One small paid offer, sellable this month. Low-medium confidence (no price evidence in this vertical for it; price is a hypothesis to test). "Pick-the-winner review": the buyer sends 10-20 finished or draft short videos/posts and the goal (reach, sales, brand). Within 48 hours they get a ranked list, the top 3 with reasons, the bottom ones with fixes in words, and what to post first. Fixed price, test at $300 to $500 per batch for a brand or agency, and a lower trial at $99 for the first 3 buyers. Evidence for the price band: Stack Influence plans of $499 to $6k/mo and Podawaa tiers of EUR 59 to 159/mo show that teams pay in this range. Afterwards compare the pick to real results and keep the table: this becomes the proof the combined company needs.

(4) Confidence summary.

  • Scheduling is verified and crowded: high.
  • Creator marketplace revenue exists: high.
  • Funders are betting on virality prediction: medium (aggregators).
  • Paying demand for scoring from small buyers: low (one data point, $2).
  • The offer in (3) sells at the stated price: low until tested.
