Answers for candidate 1

  1. What buyers pay for today. They pay for execution and outcome: ad-account management (Adside $190-290/brand/mo; managed 10% of spend, $3,500 min), revenue share (Light Anchor 5-15%), and clip/creator distribution campaigns (Clouted, quote-only). Intelligence platforms (Gudea) are funded but have no public price or customer. Confidence: MED for prices (company pages), LOW for who actually pays, since none show verified revenue.

  2. Evidence anyone pays for an independent verdict before spend. Yes, but only in the old research form: panel ad pre-testing (Zappi-type studies quoted at about $3,000-$25,000+ per study, and $2,500-$5,000 for a lower-cost option, from a competitor's blog and Zappi help pages, not price lists). LOW-MED. For a small, fast, human-judged go/test/kill verdict, I found no verified sale. At the cheap end, a $9-19 AI scorer earned $1.50. No evidence for a price in between. Absence from a US-only search is not proof of absence.

  3. Three product shapes and first paid offers (prices are hypotheses, not evidence).

    • A. Pick-the-winner review: brand or agency sends 10-20 drafts and a goal; you return rank, top 3 with reasons, kill list in 48h. First offer: $300-500 per batch, first 3 buyers at $99, sold to the 3 agencies' existing clients.
    • B. Go / test / kill gate for a campaign, sold to a celebrity or brand team before a launch budget is released: a one-page verdict plus a small test plan. First offer: $1,500 flat for one campaign decision, with a refund if the team did not act on it.
    • C. Verdict-then-run bundle for agencies: the verdict is a fee, and the test spend is run through their existing tools (e.g. Adside or Clouted style execution). First offer: $2,000/month retainer for 4 verdicts, billed to the agency.

  4. Single biggest assumption to test: that a buyer with real spend will pay a stand-alone fee for a human-judged verdict from the owner, and then act on it, rather than getting it free inside an agency or tool. Test: put offer A in front of 10 existing clients of the three agencies and count paid yes versus free interest, then compare the picks with real results. Confidence it is the right assumption: MED.

  5. Confidence labels are inline above. Overall: the funding signal is real (HIGH: Clouted, Gudea round sizes, MED); paid demand for a verdict is unproven (LOW). Every one of the four sells execution or monitoring, so a verdict-only firm is an open slot or a sign of no demand; the 48-hour test cannot tell which, only the owner's buyers can.
