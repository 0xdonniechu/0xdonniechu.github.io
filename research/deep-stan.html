Stan (stan.store), first look (2026-10-31)

Trigger: owner remarked on Stan as a link-in-bio business at "$76M". The figure was not found; sources say less. Estimates below are third-party, not company disclosures.

ARR, by source

  • ARR Club (/stan): $30M (2024-09-10), $40M with 100K+ creators (2026-05-11). Unverified; the site credits itself as the source. Page shows "+0%" growth, a placeholder. Implied roughly 19% a year (my calculation).
  • Sacra (estimate): $14.7M (2023), $28.3M (end 2024, +93%), about $35M (2025, +24%), 80,000+ active creators, about $437 per creator a year. Growth slowed with one $29/month tier and zero take rate on creator sales.
  • Contrary (Sept 2026): 85K active creators as of Aug 2026; 59K+ earned money on Stan in 2025.
  • TrustMRR (trustmrr.com/startup/stan.md, read 2026-10-31): the $76M is ALL-TIME revenue, $76,627,685, not ARR. Current MRR $3,569,654 (about $42.8M a year, consistent with ARR Club's $40M), last 12 months $15,995,741, 101,590 active subscriptions, founder Vitalii Dodonov, founded 2023-04-19. Caveats from the page: Stripe key expired and revenue last synced 2026-04-20, so figures may be stale; daily rows show $0 for October 2026 and the 3- and 6-month values are $0, which conflicts with the 30-day figure and the monthly timeline (about $2.7-3.0M a month Nov 2025 to Mar 2026). Treat as medium confidence.
  • Earlier line, now resolved: No source found for $76M. Closest large number: Whop reportedly at $142M annualized in Oct 2025 (reported by a search summary, unchecked). Possible mix-ups: GMV vs revenue, or another company. Owner to confirm where $76M came from.

How it earns (from the above, confidence medium)

  • A subscription of about $29 a month per creator, no cut of sales. ARR is roughly active creators times $29 times 12 plus add-ons.
  • Product is more than a link: store, checkout, courses, email, and an AI agent (Stanley, reported over $2M revenue in March 2026 per Sacra).

Why it matters for us (hypotheses, not findings)

  • The buyer is a creator who wants income from an audience: a clear transformation, a price low enough to pay without approval, a product people can start in minutes.
  • Revenue came from solving the whole job (sell, collect, deliver), not from the link page.
  • The creator is a buyer type on our list (celebs, creators). A tool is not a verdict, but the pattern "a simple wrapper around a real income outcome" is worth testing for media/marketing.
