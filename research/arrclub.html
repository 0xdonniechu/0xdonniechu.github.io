ARR Club (arr.club), first read (2026-10-30)

Read with the fetch tool (no browser tool in this session). ARR Club is a site that tracks ARR and growth of AI/SaaS products: 1,681 products tracked, 21 curated lists, a Breakout Radar (under $20M ARR, ranked by growth multiple), New Money, Rankings, a database. Free sign-up; Pro is $299 a year. Paywalled data was not accessed.

Reliability

  • Every profile credits "Source & Created: ARR.Club". No per-company verification method or primary source is shown; milestones link to the site's own "signal" pages. Treat it as a curated, unverified tracker, below TrustMRR (Stripe-verified). (confidence: medium that the figures are roughly right, low that each is exact)
  • The "Growth" figure is labelled "Annualized CAGR" even where the page quotes a different multiple. Outrank's "$300-300k MRR" headline looks like a typo. Team size, funding and pricing are not shown on any profile read.
  • New Money list is not useful: CrowdStrike, Visma, Nebius shown as "just started making revenue".
  • Breakout Radar: about 73 companies, 39 names visible, only the top 5 show metrics. 34 more are hidden.

Profiles read

  • HeyReach (/heyreach): LinkedIn automation and multi-account outreach built for agencies. Founded 2023, founders Nick and Stefan Velkovski, described as bootstrapped. ARR $2M (Oct 2024), $6M (Jul 2025), $10M (Dec 2025), $14M (Apr 2026), $19M (Oct 2026). Agencies are the buyer, the same buyer as the owner's three companies.
  • Submagic (/submagic): AI captions, emojis and B-roll for short video. France, founded 2023. $1M ARR (Aug 2024) to $8M (Jun 2025). Newest ARR on the page is over a year old.
  • Outrank (/outrank): AI SEO and content platform. UK, listed as est. 2018. $1M (Aug 2025), $3M (Apr 2026).
  • Oleve (/oleve): "AI-first software studio" building consumer apps, products not named. US, 2024. $6M (Feb 2025), $10M (Jul 2025).

What it adds

  • A pattern that repeats: the winners with big ARR sell tools for agencies or creators (outreach, captions, SEO). None sells a verdict. Same as the earlier sweeps. (medium)
  • HeyReach shows agencies as a buyer segment with real budget, bootstrapped to $19M in about three years. Worth a teardown for candidate 4 (agency-facing product built by a small team).
  • No list on ARR Club covers small teams, bootstrapped products or solo founders; the lists lean to frontier AI labs.
